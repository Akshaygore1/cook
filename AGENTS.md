# Dough & Go

**Do not write tests. Do not add test files, test frameworks, or testing infrastructure.**

## Game Style

- A sunny, playful, single-player 3D browser game set in a miniature farm and pizzeria.
- Use a full-screen isometric world, rounded toy-like characters, soft shadows, and original procedural scenery.
- Keep the palette warm and friendly: tomato red, golden wheat, leafy green, kitchen teal, and flour cream.
- Use rounded display typography and a clean sans serif for counters and instructions.
- Let the world dominate the screen. Keep the interface compact and readable on desktop and phones, with one clear play action and visible money, carrying capacity, objectives, and upgrades.
- Make the wheat field, oven, pickup area, and customer counter distinct, with a clear walking route between them.
- Emphasize tall visible stacks of wheat and pizzas, springy pickups, ingredient transfers, coin bursts, character movement, and gentle field sway. Respect reduced-motion preferences.

## Game Mechanics

- Keep the core loop: harvest wheat → unload at the kitchen → bake pizzas → collect pizzas → sell to customers.
- Use WASD or arrow keys on desktop and a touch joystick on phones. Harvesting, unloading, collecting, and selling happen automatically through proximity.
- Limit carrying capacity and show carried items stacked on the character's back.
- Let wheat regrow so the production loop can continue.
- Use three wheat per pizza, a timed oven, and pickup storage for finished pizzas.
- Reward sales with coins that buy a larger backpack, faster baking, and faster movement. Purchases require enough coins.
- Set the first milestone at ten pizzas served, then allow continued play and upgrades.
- Save coins, upgrades, ingredients, and progress locally between visits.
- Keep pause and optional sound controls accessible.
